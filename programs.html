Program 02 · For your insurer

Four-point & wind mitigation

Insurers commonly require a four-point report on older Florida homes covering roof, electrical, plumbing and air conditioning. Wind mitigation is the one worth asking about — documenting roof attachment, shape and openings can reduce a premium meaningfully, and many owners never have it done.

  • 1 to 2 hours
  • Owners and buyers
  • Fixed fee
  • Attic access

Program 03 · Before the builder leaves

New construction & warranty

New homes have defects too, and the eleven-month inspection exists because most builder warranties run twelve. Having an independent list before that deadline is the difference between the builder fixing something and you paying for it the following year.

  • 2 to 3 hours
  • New build owners
  • Quoted on size
  • Builder notified

Program 04 · Know before they do

Pre-listing & re-inspection

Finding the problems before a buyer's inspector does puts a seller in a far better position — fix it, price it in, or disclose it, rather than renegotiating under pressure. Re-inspections verify that agreed repairs were actually done, which is not always the case.
